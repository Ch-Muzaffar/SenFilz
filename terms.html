Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. What SenFilz does

SenFilz is a browser-based file transfer tool. When you use SenFilz, a direct peer-to-peer connection is established between two browsers using WebRTC. Files transfer directly between those two browsers.

Our servers only relay the connection details (called SDP and ICE candidates) needed to set up the direct link. We never receive, store, read, or inspect the content of any files transferred through SenFilz. Transfer codes are kept in memory for up to 10 minutes and then discarded automatically.

2. Acceptable use

By using SenFilz you agree not to use the service to transfer:

  • Content that is illegal in your jurisdiction or the recipient's jurisdiction

  • Malware, ransomware, spyware, or any software designed to damage or gain unauthorised access to systems
  • Material that infringes a third party's intellectual property rights

You are solely responsible for the files you choose to send. SenFilz does not monitor transfers and cannot prevent prohibited content from being sent. Misuse may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
